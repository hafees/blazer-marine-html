Shafts

We offer a comprehensive range of propeller shafts in various diameters and lengths to suit both leisure and commercial vessels. Customers may choose either fully finished assemblies or un-machined shaft materials, all of which can be produced under major class approvals (IACS societies such as ABS, BV, LR, and more). Each shaft is precision press-straightened and delivered as a complete assembly—including nuts, keys, and half-couplings—for seamless integration.

Materials
  • F51 Duplex high-performance stainless steel
  • 316 Stainless Steel for traditional applications

Other materials available upon request

Brackets and Struts

Propeller Shaft Support Brackets

Designed to withstand both normal and maximum engine loads, our shaft support brackets ensure dependable stability for your propeller shaft. To guarantee optimal performance, we carefully assess working loads and select the most suitable materials—typically NiBrAl or manganese bronze alloys—paired with water-lubricated bearings as standard.

We can manufacture glass-type and platform-type P and V brackets tailored to your specific requirements.

Rudders

Blazer Marine manufactures steel, stainless steel and bronze rudder assemblies, complete with tiller arms, bearings, and tubes, ensuring durability and precise maneuverability for all vessel types.

We offer custom rudder assemblies in a variety of materials to meet specific strength and corrosion resistance requirements:

  • Manganese Bronze (HTB1) – Durable and impact-resistant
  • Nickel Aluminium Bronze (AB2) – High-strength and corrosion-resistant
  • Stainless Steel – Excellent durability in marine environments

Sterntubes

Our GRP sterntubes come equipped with watertight shaft seals and bearings, ensuring leak-proof performance and smooth operation. Each sterntube is supplied with all necessary fastenings and fittings for seamless installation.

We also offer custom sterntubes in bronze and stainless steel, with lubrication options available for both oil and water to suit various vessel requirements.

Rope Cutter

Blazer Marine offers Rope Cutters, the industry-leading solution for preventing rope, fishing lines, and debris from entangling your propeller and shaft. Designed for both powerboats and sailboats, these cutters provide automatic cutting action, ensuring uninterrupted propulsion and reducing costly downtime.

  • Efficiently cuts through ropes, nets, and debris
  • Prevents entanglement-related engine strain and damage
  • Durable, corrosion-resistant construction for long-lasting performance
  • Available for both shaft and outdrive applications

Ensure a safer and more reliable voyage—contact us today to equip your vessel with a Rope Cutter!

Shaft Couplings, Flexible Couplings and Engine Mounts

Blazer Marine supplies precision-machined shaft couplings to fit the output flange of any gearbox, ensuring exceptional alignment and durability. When paired with our shafting solutions, couplings are factory-fitted to achieve optimal taper contact for maximum efficiency.

Our flexible couplings incorporate a rubber isolation element, acting as a vibration-dampening barrier between the transmission and propeller shaft. This results in reduced drivetrain noise, enhanced efficiency, and backlash-free power transmission.

Additionally, we supply engine mounts tailored to engine size and application requirements, ensuring stability, longevity, and reduced vibration in every installation.
