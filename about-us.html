About Us

Blazer Marine LLC, established in 2012 in Dubai, is a wholly independent, UAE-based entity with strong local and international affiliations. We are an ISO 9001:2015–certified marine propulsion manufacturer, equipment supplier, and service provider, operating from our showroom and manufacturing facility in Al Jadaf Docking yard. Our unwavering dedication to the regional leisure and commercial marine industry shines through in our comprehensive portfolio of products and services.

In a rapidly evolving maritime landscape, Blazer Marine stands out as a forward-thinking, technology-driven solutions provider that offers exceptional value to local and regional clients. Backed by decades of experience, we swiftly identify and address customer needs, offering optimal solutions with unrivalled efficiency. We excel in turnkey specialized technical projects, supported by a dedicated team of engineers, naval architects, and service professionals available 24/7, 365 days a year, ensuring responsive support and unmatched after-sales service.

At Blazer Marine, our ethos are perfect for time-critical ship operations and we work closely with major IACS (International Association of Classification Societies) members—such as ABS, RINA, Lloyd’s Register, Bureau Veritas, DNV, IRS, and TASNEEF—underscoring our commitment to upholding the highest industry standards.

As proud distributors, we collaborate with esteemed industry leaders, including Yanmar, ZF, Lecomble & Schmitt, Halyard, Lewmar, Maxpower, Lofrans, Tides Marine Sure Seals, PSS Seals, QS Seamaster, Tufcot and Timken. These alliances enable us to address a broad spectrum of marine requirements, reinforcing our dedication to strengthening the maritime ecosystem.
