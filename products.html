Composite Bearings (Tufcot)

Engineered for the marine environment, Tufcot® T100 offers exceptional durability, low friction, and corrosion resistance. These bearings can operate with water, oil, or grease lubrication and even function in dry-running conditions.

Why Choose Tufcot®?

  • Low Friction, High Efficiency – Friction as low as 0.04, reducing energy consumption and extending bearing life.
  • Self-Lubricating – No need for oil, grease, or water lubrication, minimizing maintenance and environmental impact.
  • Minimal Swell & Dimensional Stability – Negligible water absorption, ensuring precise tolerances and marine class approvals (ABS, BV, DNV, LR, RINA).
  • High Strength & Impact Resistance – Withstands overloads, impact, and emergency conditions without permanent deformation.
  • Lightweight & Easy Installation – Six times lighter than metal bearings, with press-fit, freeze-fit, or adhesive bonding options.
  • Extended Service Life – Durable, wear-resistant, and eliminates frequent lubrication and inspections.

With BV Mode II Scheme recognition and global stock availability, Tufcot® bearings are ready for urgent same-day delivery—ensuring maximum reliability and minimal downtime.

Blazer Marine – Your Trusted Source for Tufcot® Bearings.

Cutlass Bearing
